# Brand context — CometChat (default)

Default brand for relevance judgement, the beats to monitor, and the CometChat angle on each story.
Override if the roundup is for a different property.

## Product

**CometChat** is a chat & calling SDK/API developers drop into web and mobile apps — one-to-one and
group **messaging**, **voice/video calling**, and **moderation/AI** features, with UI Kits and SDKs
for React, React Native, Flutter, iOS/Android, Angular, JavaScript, and more.

## Audience (ICP)

Developers and product/eng leaders building in-app chat, live video, dating, social, community,
marketplace, healthcare, gaming, or support experiences. The roundup should speak to *building*
(implementation, SDKs, architecture) and *evaluating* (comparisons, pricing, alternatives) — not
generic consumer-app news.

## Competitors (track their news; never position as a CometChat property)

Sendbird, Stream (GetStream), PubNub, Twilio (Conversations / Video), Ably, ZEGOCLOUD, Agora,
MirrorFly, TalkJS, Firebase (as a build-your-own alternative). Their launches, funding, pricing
changes, M&A, and positioning shifts are the core of the CI watch.

- Tone: clear, technical, developer-credible; no hype, no clickbait.
- US English by default.

## News beats (what this skill monitors)

The default beats expand from the product + competitor set above:

1. **Competitor moves** — launches, releases/changelogs, funding rounds, M&A, pricing/positioning
   changes from the competitor list.
2. **Chat & messaging SDKs/APIs** — new SDKs, libraries, protocol/feature news in in-app messaging.
3. **Voice/video & WebRTC** — calling features, WebRTC and real-time-media standards and platform news.
4. **AI & moderation** — AI in chat (agents, copilots, summarization), content moderation, trust &
   safety tooling — a fast-moving, high-priority beat for CometChat.
5. **Platform SDKs** — React, React Native, Flutter, iOS/Android, Angular, JavaScript releases that
   affect how developers build chat/calling.
6. **Industry & standards** — funding climate, acquisitions, regulation, and standards (e.g. messaging
   interop) shaping the space.

Down-weight: generic consumer messaging-app gossip, unrelated big-tech news, and anything that doesn't
touch how developers build chat/calling.
