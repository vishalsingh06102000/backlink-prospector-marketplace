# Drafting the outreach email

Write one short, genuinely personalized email per prospect. These are **drafts for human review** —
never send them. The whole point is that the recipient can tell a real person read their page.

## Rules

- **Reference their specific page** naturally, to prove it was actually read. Generic "I loved your
  blog" reads as spam.
- **Be concise** — roughly 90–150 words. No fluff, no fake flattery, no spammy superlatives.
- **One clear, low-friction ask**, based on the campaign's call to action (suggest a resource link,
  pitch a guest post, ask to be added to a roundup, etc.).
- **Plain text**, no markdown. Use the sender's name/company in the signature.
- **End with one identification + opt-out line** — who you are + a polite "if this isn't relevant,
  just let me know and I won't follow up." This is basic CAN-SPAM / GDPR hygiene and also just
  decent.
- Match the requested **tone**: friendly / professional / concise / enthusiastic. Default friendly.
- Address the contact by first name if known; otherwise a simple "Hi there".

## Per-prospect inputs you have

The prospect's ranking page (title + what it's about), why it matched, the product/offer and its
asset URL, the call to action, and the sender details. Use the page specifics to open the email.

## Output

For each prospect produce a **subject** (short, specific, not clickbait) and a **body**. Put them in
the Sheet's `Draft subject` and `Draft email` columns.

## Worked example

Prospect: `smashingmagazine.com`, article "Building Real-Time Chat Apps". Product: CometChat (chat &
calling SDK). Offer: free tutorial library. CTA: suggest the tutorial as a resource link. Tone:
friendly. Sender: Vishal, CometChat.

**Subject:** A hands-on chat SDK resource for your real-time apps guide

**Body:**
```
Hi Vitaly,

Really enjoyed your piece on building real-time chat apps — the section on presence and
reconnection logic is something most tutorials skip.

We build CometChat, a chat & calling SDK developers drop into web and mobile apps. We put together
a free tutorial library that walks through exactly those edge cases — it might be a useful resource
link for readers who want to go from concept to working chat quickly:
https://www.cometchat.com/tutorials

Would you be open to including it where it fits?

Thanks for the great work,
Vishal
Growth, CometChat

If this isn't relevant, just let me know and I won't follow up.
```

Notice: it names a specific detail from the page, makes one easy ask, stays short, and ends with a
clean identification + opt-out. Mirror that shape; vary the wording per prospect so they don't read
as a mail-merge.
